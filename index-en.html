This atlas organizes embodied models by the role of their modules and interfaces. Start with a family, inspect a representative system, and follow the original paper for exact equations, datasets, ablations, and evaluation details.

How to use this edition. The English page is a structured orientation layer covering 8 core architectures, 18 orthogonal mechanisms, and 38 representative models. Figure links, paper sources, training/deployment boundaries, and evidence notes remain attached to every entry. It is a guide into the literature, not a substitute for the paper.
