# Contributing

Thank you for helping improve the Embodied Model Architecture Atlas.

## What is useful

- A missing influential embodied-AI model or method
- A correction to a category, interface, training/deployment description, or result
- The original paper figure or official project link for an entry
- A translation improvement or a clearer beginner-facing explanation
- A suggestion for a new architecture family

## How to propose a change

Open an Issue with the smallest useful evidence. Include the paper title, arXiv or official URL, figure/section number when relevant, and a short explanation of the proposed change. For larger edits, open a pull request and explain which page or data entry changed.

Please keep the evidence boundary explicit. Reported paper results, author claims, community reports, and atlas-level interpretations should not be mixed together. Do not upload copyrighted paper PDFs or figures without a link and the required attribution.

## Review standard

The goal is a readable orientation layer that sends readers back to primary sources. A change is ready when its wording is precise, its links work, its figure attribution is present, and the Chinese and English pages remain structurally aligned.
